Don't miss anything that matters to you.

Hi everyone,

Deadlines and opportunities at Masters' Union often arrive through different places: email, WhatsApp groups, the LMS, Coach, calendars and other portals. Keeping track of everything can become complicated, and important deadlines, sessions or opportunities can easily get missed.

I have faced this personally, and I believe many of you may relate to it.

That is why I started building MU One: a single student portal designed around one simple idea:
Don't miss anything.

With MU One, you will be able to:

  • Track deadlines and important updates in one place.
  • View classes across Sections 1–10.
  • Find another section conducting the same subject if you miss a session, including its date, time and venue, subject to the relevant academic permissions.
  • Create anonymous or public surveys to validate ideas and receive more honest responses.
  • Check whether a pitch deck actually addresses the given problem, evaluation criteria and expected outcomes.
  • Import a spreadsheet to prepare personalized bulk email campaigns with subjects, attachments and scheduled delivery.
  • Access new AI-assisted workflows as they are released.

Coming in later phases:

  • WhatsApp reminders for deadlines and important events.
  • A voice assistant that helps organize your day and tasks.
  • Founder Connect, where students can share their interests, experience and working styles to discover potential co-founders and receive a compatibility score.
  • More tools for pitch preparation, opportunity tracking and student productivity.

Join the private beta

We are opening MU One gradually. Access will be granted in phases to selected email addresses so that we can collect feedback, improve reliability and understand which features students need most.
